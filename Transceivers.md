### Transceivers

A [[#^019730|transceiver]] is any device that can both transmit and receive data. Transceivers are considered [[Layer 1 (Physical Layer)|Layer 1]] devices.

Transceivers can:
* Convert data sent by one protocol into another protocol
* Convert network connections between different media types, even when the protocol is the same, acting sort of like a media converter

Ex. converting light signals from ST-based fiber into electrical signals to transmit over CAT 6, or converting a long-distance fiber connection back into copper media for use in buildings.

Each transceiver uses a specific protocol. The two main protocols are:
* [[#^ac36de|Ethernet]]
* [[#^271424|Fibre Channel]]

### Form Factors

Transceivers come in different form factors, and all of them are hot pluggable:

| Form factor | Speed |
| --- | --- |
| [[#^797a5c\|SFP]] | 4.25 Gbps |
| [[#^e3b5ae\|SFP+]] | 16 Gbps |
| [[#^cac6fd\|QSFP]] | 4 Gbps |
| [[#^42e2df\|QSFP+]] | 40 Gbps |
| [[#^cd5683\|QSFP28]] | 100 Gbps |
| [[#^c451b6\|QSFP56]] | 200 Gbps |

NOTE: Dion says the speeds do not need to be memorized for the exam. You do need to remember that QSFP is faster than SFP, and that all of these are just different transceivers that convert light from fiber back to electrical signals for use with copper media again.

### Glossary

Transceiver: Any device that can both transmit and receive data. Can convert data from one protocol to another, or convert connections between media types, like fiber to copper. Considered a Layer 1 device. ^019730

Ethernet: Family of computer networking technologies commonly used in LANs, MANs, and WANs. ^ac36de

Fibre Channel: High-speed network protocol primarily used to connect computer data storage to servers in a storage area network. Handles multiple Gbps and can support fiber and copper media. High throughput, preferred for enterprise. ^271424

Small Form-factor Pluggable (SFP): 4.25 Gbps. Hot pluggable. Single channel. ^797a5c

Small Form-factor Pluggable Plus (SFP+): 16 Gbps. Hot pluggable. ^e3b5ae

Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable (QSFP): 4 Gbps. Compact and hot pluggable. ^cac6fd

Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable Plus (QSFP+): 40 Gbps. Compact and hot pluggable. ^42e2df

Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable 28 (QSFP28): 100 Gbps. Compact and hot pluggable. ^cd5683

Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable 56 (QSFP56): 200 Gbps. Compact and hot pluggable. ^c451b6
