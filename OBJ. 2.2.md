* [[Ethernet Fundamentals]]: CSMA/CD, collision and broadcast domains, speed and duplex, MTU and jumbo frames
* [[VLANs]]: VLANs, the VLAN database, SVIs, 802.1Q tagging, trunking, native and voice VLANs, link aggregation
* [[Spanning Tree Protocol]]: root bridges, port roles, link cost, port states
