### Routing Technologies

This objective covers the characteristics of routing technologies. It's split into four notes:
* [[Routing Fundamentals]]: how routers move traffic, routing tables, route selection and administrative distance, route types, and preventing routing loops
* [[Routing Protocols]]: interior vs. exterior protocols, advertisement methods, convergence, RIP, OSPF, IS-IS, EIGRP, BGP, multicast routing, and GRE
* [[Address Translation]]: NAT, PAT, and the inside/outside local/global address names
* [[Routing Redundancy]]: FHRP, virtual IPs, subinterfaces, HSRP, VRRP, and GLBP
