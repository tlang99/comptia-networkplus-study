### Media and Cabling

[[#^7c1e40|Media]] is what carries data between devices. The common types of media are:
* [[Copper Cabling|Copper]] (cables, connectors, and building a copper cable)
* [[Fiber Cabling|Fiber]] (single-mode and multi-mode, connectors, and polish types)
* Radio Frequencies

### Transceivers

[[Transceivers]] transmit and receive data, and convert between protocols and media types, like fiber to copper. Covers the SFP and QSFP form factors.

### Glossary

Media: Physical material used to transfer data between devices. ^7c1e40
